# Creative OS — Sovereign Operating System for Creative Entrepreneurs

> **Creative OS (v2.2.0)** — Designed & Architected by **Brian Etole** (Director, Cinematographer & Creative Technologist based in Nairobi, Kenya).
> Built as an offline-first, sovereign operating system for directors, visual storytellers, filmmakers, photographers, and independent creative studios.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture & Core Philosophy
1. **100% On-Device Sovereignty:** Zero cloud tracking, zero external telemetry, zero subscription lock-in. Powered by high-speed native SQLite via Drift.
2. **Multi-Doorway Navigation (The Hub):**
   - **Production:** Call sheets, gear manifests, crew rosters, and live production handover portals.
   - **Stage & Card:** 3D gyro-responsive digital cards, NFC beam, vCard export, and 10-per-A4 print-ready PDF generator.
   - **Network & Rolodex:** Venue memory, on-device OCR business card scanner, and physical occasion tracking.
   - **Docs & Finance:** Branded quotations, invoices, receipts, rate cards, and legal contracts with dynamic `{PREFIX}-{NUM}` numbering and tap-to-edit.
   - **Meetings & Memory:** AI speech ingestion (on-device Whisper), SMS/call log parsing, action item extraction, and WhatsApp chaser engine.
   - **Settings & Studio Config:** Brand identity, SMTP direct mail dispatch, theme customizer, and full backup/restore.
