# Etole — User Guide & Quick Reference

Welcome to **Etole** (v2.0.0), your personal, offline-first creative business suite designed specifically for hybrid filmmaking and photography workflows in Nairobi and beyond.

---

## 1. Brian Tab (Your Digital Identity)
* **Interactive 3D Business Card:** Tap the card to flip between Front and Back. Drag with your finger to tilt the card with realistic 3D perspective and watch the dynamic light sheen glint across the tribal pattern.
* **5 East African Tribal Styles:** Choose between `Ember` (Maasai shúkà diagonals), `Savannah` (Acacia diamond thorn lattice), `Ochre Line` (stepped African glyphs), `Night Market` (obsidian & neon zigzag), and `Clay` (Rift Valley beads).
* **QR Mode Switcher:** Tap the quick-mode chips below your card to instantly switch the QR code between:
  - **Save Contact:** Generates an offline `.vcf` vCard contact.
  - **WhatsApp Me:** Pre-fills a direct WhatsApp message to you.
  - **Watch Reel:** Opens your showreel (`https://brianetole.com`).
  - **Custom Link:** Directs to any project portfolio or URL you specify.
* **NFC Writing:** Tap **NFC Tag** to encode physical NFC tags (cards, keychains, phone stickers) with your contact info or reel.
* **10-per-A4 Print PDF:** Tap **Print Cards PDF** to generate print-ready vector PDF sheets with 3mm bleed, crop marks, and safe area guides.
* **Email Signature:** Tap **Email Signature** to generate rich HTML or clean plain-text signatures for Gmail and Outlook.

---

## 2. Network Tab (Who You Meet & Where)
* **People & Leads:**
  - **On-Device OCR Business Card Scanning:** Tap **Scan Card (OCR)** to photograph any physical business card. On-device machine learning reads the text and presents a verification screen with pinch-to-zoom to confirm Name, Title, Company, Phone, Email, and Website.
  - **Phone Contacts Import:** Import contacts directly from your device phonebook.
  - **Send Intro:** 1-tap to start a WhatsApp chat or send an introduction email.
  - **Convert to Client:** Tap **Convert to Client** on any lead to promote them to your active client directory and immediately begin a quotation or job.
* **Occasions & Events:**
  - Register festivals, sets, conferences, or casual meetings.
  - Turn on **Event Mode** to automatically tag every new lead you meet with that occasion.

---

## 3. Docs Tab (Creative Business Documents)
* **Strictly 5 Essential Documents:**
  1. **Quotation:** Estimate creative fees and line items.
  2. **Invoice:** Bill clients for milestones or completed deliveries.
  3. **Receipt:** Official proof of payment with settlement note.
  4. **Rate Card:** Standard commercial day rates and equipment packages.
  5. **Contract:** Dual-signed legal agreements with reusable clauses.
* **1-Tap Sequential Conversion:**
  - `Quotation` &rarr; `Invoice` &rarr; `Receipt`
  - `Rate Card` &rarr; `Quotation`
* **Signed Copy Camera Scanner:** Take a photo of physically counter-signed paper contracts and attach them directly to the record.
* **PDF Preview & Export:** Live vector PDF generation with sharing to WhatsApp, Email, or Print.

---

## 4. Settings Tab
* **Identity:** Edit your bio, tagline, and contact info.
* **Payment Details:** Add your M-Pesa Till / Paybill and Bank details once; they automatically appear on all invoice and quotation footers.
* **Appearance & Display:**
  - Toggle between **OLED Pure Black**, **Cinematic Dark**, and **Warm Light** modes.
  - **Reduce Motion:** Disable 3D tilt animations for battery saving or accessibility.
* **Offline Backup:** Full offline backup and restore (`.etole` / `.zip`) with automatic 7-backup rolling retention.
* **Trash:** Deleted documents and contacts are safely stored for 30 days before permanent purging.
